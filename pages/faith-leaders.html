What the letter asks

Three requests

When you sign, you add your voice to these three requests.

  1. 01

    Religious freedom in occupied Ukraine

    Demand that Russia allow religious freedom in the occupied parts of Ukraine, including lifting its restrictions on worship.

  2. 02

    Bring the children home

    Demand the return of every Ukrainian child abducted by Russia.

  3. 03

    Protect churches and civilians

    Help shield Ukraine's people and places of worship from missile and drone attacks.
