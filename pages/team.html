Our team

The people behind the films

Filmmakers, veterans, policy experts and a chaplain, working on the ground in Ukraine to tell the story of faith under siege.

Meet the team

  • Producer · Executive Producer

    Colby Barrett, JD, PE

    Entrepreneur and filmmaker. A former U.S. Marine Corps captain, he led infantry and scout/sniper platoons in the Pacific Rim and the Middle East.

  • Executive Producer

    Steven E. Moore

    A former chief of staff in the U.S. House of Representatives, he has spent most of his time in Ukraine since the fifth day of the war. He leads the Ukraine Freedom Project, which has delivered 250 tons of food, medical supplies, body armor and other aid to the front.

  • Executive Producer

    Anna Shvetsova

    COO of the Ukraine Freedom Project. Raised near the Russian border, she visits the front lines often and, as a U.S.–Ukraine policy expert, has met with more than 100 Congressional offices.

  • Special Forces Veteran

    Christian Hickey

    Founder and CEO of Triple A Authority Foundation. After 15 years in the U.S. military, as a Marine and a Green Beret, and in law enforcement, he brings that experience to global missions and serves on the front lines in Ukraine.

  • Impact Producer

    Sarah Makin

    Former Deputy Assistant to President Trump, Senior Director on the National Security Council and the President's Senior Advisor on International Religious Freedom. She was also Director of Strategic Engagement and External Affairs for Vice President Mike Pence, and continues to advocate for persecuted religious minorities worldwide.

  • Chaplain

    Karl Ahlgren

    A licensed chaplain and veteran political advisor with more than 40 years in government and advocacy. Since 2023 he has made several trips to Ukraine to provide humanitarian support and amplify the voices of persecuted believers.

  • Film Director

    Yaroslav Lodygin

    Ukrainian film director, producer and screenwriter, known for his award-winning debut feature The Wild Fields (2018).

  • Producer

    Charlie Sadoff

    Producer, director and editor. His most recent film, Against All Enemies, premiered to strong reviews at the 2023 Tribeca Film Festival.
