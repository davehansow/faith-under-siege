Why share it

Bring the story to your community

A Faith Under Siege tells the true story of Christians in Ukraine who face violent persecution for their faith. We're making the film free to download so organizations and outlets everywhere can spread its message of courage, prayer and unshakable hope.

This isn't just about watching a film. It's about using your voice and your platform to:

Questions

Why host it yourself?

Why host the film on my own website or channel instead of sharing a link?

Hosting gives you full ownership of the message. Your audience sees the film directly from a trusted source aligned with your mission, not through a third-party link, which builds deeper trust and engagement.

Can I customize how the film is presented?

Yes. You can add introductions, calls to action, prayer and discussion guides and other materials as links on your A Faith Under Siege player page. If you schedule a screening for your audience, you can host a follow-up discussion and Q&A on Zoom or another video call service.

How does hosting improve audience connection?

When content lives within your trusted community, people engage more fully, with longer watch times, richer conversations and stronger responses.

How does hosting affect my ministry's role?

Hosting makes you an active leader in the movement supporting persecuted Ukrainian believers, and shows your commitment in a visible, meaningful way.

Would you rather show it to a group in person?

We can help with that too. Sign up to host a screening and we'll send a free screening kit.
