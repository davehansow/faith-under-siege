Even in the Ruins

Faith in War · Film 2 of 5 · 2026 · 31 min · Documentary

After Russian forces seize his church, Ukrainian pastor Mark Sergeev keeps his congregation together.

Mark led Melitopol Christian Church, founded by his parents more than 30 years ago, until Russian forces confiscated the church and his home. After moving to Kyiv, he lost another home to the war.

Even in the Ruins follows the pastor and frontline chaplain as he rebuilds his ministry and keeps serving soldiers, displaced families and communities devastated by Russia’s invasion. It is film two of the Faith in War series.

Featuring Mark Sergeev.
