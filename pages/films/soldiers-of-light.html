Soldiers of Light

Faith in War · Film 4 of 5 · 2026 · 36 min · Documentary

Christian Hickey, a former Marine, deputy sheriff and Green Beret, moves his family to Ukraine to serve its soldiers as a missionary.

Most families would do everything they could to stay away from a war. Christian and Ashley Hickey moved toward one, with their five children beside them, answering what they believed was God’s call after Russia’s full-scale invasion.

Guided by a Ukrainian military chaplain, Christian travels toward the front lines to deliver humanitarian aid and medical supplies and to hand out audio Bibles. Soldiers of Light is film four of the Faith in War series.
