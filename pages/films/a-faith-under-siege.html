A Faith Under Siege: Russia’s Hidden War on Ukraine’s Christians

Feature documentary · 2025 · 58 min · Documentary

A Faith Under Siege goes inside occupied Ukraine, where pastors are hunted, churches are seized, and believers keep worshipping anyway.

In a region once known as the post-Soviet “Bible Belt,” evangelical and Protestant Christians now face seized churches, tortured pastors and abducted children under Russian occupation. Yet their faith is not fading. It is growing stronger.

Through firsthand accounts and footage filmed on the ground, the documentary shows that this is not only a war for land. It is a war for faith. The full film is free to watch on YouTube, and churches and groups can host a free screening.
