The Stolen Children

Faith in War · Film 1 of 5 · 2026 · 39 min · Documentary

Veteran Meaghan Mobbs searches for the Ukrainian children Russia has abducted, and the families still waiting for them.

Ukraine has verified more than 20,000 cases of children taken by Russia. Many have had their names changed and their language forbidden, and some have been told their families no longer want them.

The Stolen Children follows Mobbs, a combat veteran and West Point graduate, as she travels to Ukraine to investigate one of the most devastating and underreported consequences of the war, working alongside Ukrainian officials and human-rights investigators. It is film one of the Faith in War series.
