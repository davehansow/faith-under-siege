No Surrender

Faith in War · Film 5 of 5 · 2026 · 18 min · Documentary

When Russian forces invaded Ukraine, Kyiv pastor Yuri Babinets had every reason to leave. He chose to stay.

No Surrender follows Yuri through the first uncertain days of the full-scale invasion and the long war that followed. As missiles fell and families fled, he kept his church doors open and served people with nowhere else to turn.

His courage is the kind that rarely makes headlines: staying present, sitting with families who have lost everything, and showing up when leaving would be easier. It is film five of the Faith in War series, also available in Ukrainian.
