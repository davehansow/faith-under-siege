The Pastor of Kherson

2025 · 12 min · Documentary

When war reached the streets of Kherson, Ukrainian pastor Oleh Derkachenko faced a choice: surrender to fear or stand firm in faith.

This award-winning short tells the true story of a man whose journey from addiction to redemption prepared him for an extraordinary test of courage. Through powerful storytelling and dramatic recreations, it follows a pastor determined to lead his church, serve his neighbors and hold on to hope in the face of overwhelming danger.
