Safe Ground

Faith in War · Film 3 of 5 · 2026 · 28 min · Documentary

Former Green Beret Ryan Hendrickson returns to Ukraine to clear landmines from farmland, so families can plant, harvest and live on their land again.

In 2010, during combat operations in Afghanistan, Hendrickson stepped on an IED while trying to rescue an Afghan soldier. He recovered, returned to active duty, and later carried that experience into a new mission.

Safe Ground follows the retired U.S. Army Green Beret, Purple Heart recipient and author as he risks his life so Ukrainians can walk their land safely again. It is film three of the Faith in War series, and it also streams on Prime Video.
