No Priests Left

Dive Deeper shorts · 16 min · Documentary

When Russian forces occupy his city, a Ukrainian Catholic priest refuses to flee and keeps serving his parishioners under persecution.

No Priests Left asks what happens to a parish when every priest is gone, and shows both the brutality of the occupation and the strength of the faith that survives it.
