Questions

Good to know

Does it cost anything to host?

No. The screening kit is free, including the film, bonus videos and promotional materials.

How big does my group need to be?

Any size works, from a small group in a living room to a public venue.

How long is the film?

A Faith Under Siege runs about 60 minutes. Plan extra time for the bonus videos and discussion.

What happens after I sign up?

A member of our team follows up with your kit and helps you plan the screening.

Email us at hello@faithundersiege.com and we'll get back to you.
